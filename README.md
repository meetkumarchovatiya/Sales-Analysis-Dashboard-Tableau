# 📊 Sales Analysis Dashboard | Tableau

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard developed using Tableau to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, and geographical trends.

The dashboard provides key business insights through KPI tracking, year-over-year comparisons, monthly sales analysis, geographical visualizations, and sales performance by location and manager.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total sales, profit, and quantity.
- Compare current-year and previous-year performance.
- Identify monthly sales trends across customer segments.
- Analyze sales and profit performance across states.
- Compare sales performance by location and manager.
- Build an interactive dashboard using Tableau parameters and calculated fields.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Tableau Public** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** — Dataset storage and preparation
- **Calculated Fields** — KPI calculations and business logic
- **Parameters** — Dynamic measure selection
- **Data Visualization** — Interactive charts and performance analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. KPI Overview
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Year-over-year percentage change

### 2. Year-over-Year Analysis

Compare current-year (CY) and previous-year (PY) performance to understand changes in sales, profit, and quantity.

### 3. Monthly Sales by Segment

Analyze monthly sales trends across:
- Consumer
- Corporate
- Home Office

### 4. Geographical Sales and Profit Analysis

Visualize state-level sales and profit distribution to identify regional performance patterns.

### 5. Sales by Location and Manager

Compare sales performance across managers and locations to identify high-performing areas and business opportunities.

### 6. Dynamic Measure Selection

Use Tableau parameters to switch between Sales, Profit, and Quantity for flexible analysis.

## 💡 Business Questions Answered

- What are the total sales, profit, and quantity?
- How does current-year performance compare with the previous year?
- Which customer segments contribute to sales?
- Which states have higher or lower sales and profit?
- Which managers and locations generate the most sales?
- What monthly sales patterns can support business decisions?
